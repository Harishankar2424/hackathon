# **App Name**: SynergyChain

## Core Features:

- Distributor Onboarding: Streamlined signup process for distributors with Google/Email, OTP verification, and region selection.
- Vendor Onboarding: Simple signup page for vendors to enter their company information (company_email).
- Contract Summary Generation: AI tool generates a concise summary of contract key points, presenting main statistics in a tabular format, including exclusivity, territory, pricing, sales requirements, IP, terms, and confidentiality.
- Region-Based Contract Publishing: Vendors can select a region and publish contract offers, accessible only to distributors in that region.
- Distributor Offer Application: Distributors can view vendor offers and apply with company details and authentication information.
- AI-Powered Distributor Vetting: AI agent tool analyzes distributor details (region, trustworthiness, work history) and product info to suggest options for vendors, aiding in distributor selection.
- Automated Onboarding: Upon selection of offers, the AI automates official communication, distributes required resources, and invites to collaborative platforms.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: HSL(210, 60%, 50%) which converts to RGB hex #3399FF, chosen to evoke trust and reliability, vital for a platform handling sensitive contract information.
- Background color: A light theme will be used with background HSL(210, 20%, 95%), which converts to RGB hex #F0F8FF, providing a clean and professional appearance.
- Accent color: HSL(180, 50%, 40%) which converts to RGB hex #33BDBD.  This is used for secondary buttons, highlights, and calls to action, as well as visual interest and ease of navigation.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' (sans-serif) for a modern and geometric feel in titles.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) to provide clarity and legibility.
- Simple and professional icons to represent different contract aspects and regions.
- Clean, grid-based layout to ensure information is well-organized and easy to navigate for both vendors and distributors.
